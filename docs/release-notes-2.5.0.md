# QiTV 2.5.0

QiTV 2.5 turns your phone into the remote: scan a code on the TV once, and the phone's browser
drives QiTV like its keyboard, with the guide, search and your Library at hand.

**Please always download QiTV from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases)
instead of using others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## New

- **Phone remote.** Choose QiTV → Phone Remote… (it turns the remote on the first time) and
  scan the code on the TV with your phone's camera: no app to install. The arrows, OK and Back
  work as the keyboard's wherever you are, and a held button repeats like a held key and stops
  where you let go. TV, Movies and Series jump to those pages; volume, channels, play and
  pause, rewind, Go live and Mute are a thumb away, and More holds the channel numbers, Record,
  Picture in picture, Swap, Multiview, Picture and Full screen. Prefer swiping? The phone's
  menu turns the arrows into a touchpad.
- **A second screen.** The phone shows what's on with its progress; a **Guide** that opens
  where the TV's channel list is, on the channel playing, with its groups (Favorites, Recent
  and the provider's own) one level up: a channel opens its card (its timetable and Watch; a
  past programme plays from the start when the provider keeps an archive), the ▶ on its row
  tunes at once; the **Library**'s Movies and Series by genre, Continue first, then a genre's
  titles, its find box finding genres and the section's films or series alike; and the TV's
  search results as you type on the phone's keyboard. Every find box searches the whole list
  shown, 185,000 films included, and browsing on the phone leaves the TV where it is until you
  tap.
- **At home, or anywhere.** At home the phone talks to QiTV directly over Wi-Fi. With Reach QiTV
  from any network on, it also works on mobile data or another network through the QiTV relay,
  which passes along end-to-end encrypted messages it can't read; back on your Wi-Fi the phone
  switches to the direct line by itself.
- **Pairing you control.** The code on the TV changes every minute, pairs one phone and only
  works while it shows. Settings → Phone remote lists the paired phones, and Remove cuts one off
  at once. A phone can pair with several QiTVs and choose between them.

## Fixed

- **The programme guide after a restart.** When the saved guide is older than its update period,
  it now shows while the fresh one downloads, instead of no programmes for the minutes a large
  provider guide takes.
