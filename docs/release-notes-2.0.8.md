# QiTV 2.0.8

The pointer gets out of the way while you watch, and the playback speed goes both ways.

**Please always download QiTV from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases)
instead of using others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## New

- **The pointer hides while you watch.** A few seconds after you stop moving the mouse, it
  goes away together with the player controls, in a window and in fullscreen. Move the
  mouse and both come back. It stays while the channel list, the guide or the volume mixer
  is open.
- **Slower and faster.** The speed on the player controls now has **−** and **+**, from
  0.5× to 2×, for movies, episodes and rewound live TV. Click the speed itself to go back to
  normal.

## Fixes

- **The speed is readable in a small window.** "1.25x" was cut off when the window was
  small. **Go live** and **‹ Library** fit their text there too now.
