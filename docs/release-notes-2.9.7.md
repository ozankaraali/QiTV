# QiTV 2.9.7

**Download QiTV from [qitv.app](https://qitv.app), which picks the right build for your
computer, or from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases). Please don't use
others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## Recordings, a tab of their own

- **The Library's Recordings tab** (<kbd>Ctrl/⌘+5</kbd>) has everything about recording, with
  rows on the left as Movies has. It opens on **Tonight**:
  - what records now, with how far it got and when it ends (**▶ Watch**, **■ Stop**);
  - the next recording and how soon it starts;
  - **＋ At a time…**, and how many of each provider's streams are in use;
  - the guide of your favorite channels for the next hours: click a programme to record it once
    or every airing;
  - the latest recordings.
- **Its other rows:**
  - **Coming up**: every scheduled airing by day; **Try now** and **Skip** for one waiting for a
    stream, **Edit…** and **Cancel Recording**.
  - **Series & timers.**
  - **Needs attention**: missed or failed recordings, with why and what helps.
  - **All** and each channel's recordings.
  - **Folders & streams.**
- **Tiles with pictures:** each recording shows a picture taken from its file, once, quietly in
  the background; a scheduled one shows its channel's logo. Each tile has its title, channel,
  length, and **NEW** until you play it.
- **The find box** finds recordings, and programmes still to come in the guide, to record with
  one click.
- **Skip one airing of a series:** the series goes on with the next.
- **Recordings leave the Movies shelf;** each one remembers its programme, channel and logo.
  A provider with no films no longer shows a Movies tab for recordings alone.
- **In the guide, a series' airing offers Skip** (that airing only) and **Cancel series**;
  Cancel alone was the whole series.

## Recording with fewer streams

- **Watching a channel that records takes no second stream:** picture in picture, Go back to,
  and Watch on a recording now play the recording's own buffer.
- **A programme due on the channel you watch records from your player,** from its start as far
  back as the buffer goes: no stream of its own. Until now a one-stream account waited for a
  free stream until the programme was over.
- **When the provider breaks the stream** under a recording, the rest of the programme still
  records; the part before it is kept.
- **Skipping an airing ends it everywhere,** even one already waiting for a stream or on its way.
- **The page stays quick with hundreds of recordings.**
- **Quitting while something records** keeps its real length on its tile.
- **Record at a time…** opens at once with every provider's channels, and of two channels with
  one name, records the one you chose.

## Phone remote

- **In the TV app's own look:** the channel menu's numbers, logo tiles in the TV's own colours,
  the orange progress line, the warm "chosen" row, the Library's cards and tabs, and a flat
  d-pad.
- **Recordings on the phone open on one page,** as Tonight does: what records now (**Watch** on
  the TV, **Stop**), the next airings (tap one for **Try now**, **Skip** or **Cancel
  Recording**), the latest recordings, and **All recordings ›**. A recording plays on the phone.
- **A switch says where a tap plays,** at the top of the Guide and the Library: off on the TV,
  on (**Phone**) on the phone. **Show on TV** in Search is the same switch.

## Playing

- **The mouse wheel over the picture changes the volume;** over the channel list it still moves
  through the channels. Everyone moves to the volume once, even if you had chosen channels:
  **Settings → Mouse wheel** brings them back over the picture.
- **Picture in picture takes its picture's shape:** no bars beside a 4:3 channel, in the small
  picture and the floating window.
- **A channel the provider refuses (403 or 407)** now says it was refused, instead of "couldn't
  open", and is no longer asked again over HTTP when its HTTPS address refused.
