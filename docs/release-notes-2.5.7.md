# QiTV 2.5.7

**Download QiTV from [qitv.app](https://qitv.app), which picks the right build for your
computer, or from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases). Please don't use
others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## New

- **Films and episodes on the phone.** Watching on the phone, the Library's films and episodes
  play there too, as channels do; a series opens its seasons on the TV as before. The player
  stays above whichever tab you're in, and the Remote tab's Channels, the Guide and the Library
  each have the TV | Phone switch. Tap TV to hand a film back to the TV. MKV films may not play
  on an iPhone.
- **Search reaches every provider.** With two providers or more, the phone's "Find a channel"
  finds channels of every provider without going up to All providers: the open group's own
  first, then the rest of the provider you're in, then the others.
