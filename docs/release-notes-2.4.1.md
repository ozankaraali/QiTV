# QiTV 2.4.1

**Please always download QiTV from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases)
instead of using others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## Changed

- **The floating picture resizes (Mac).** Drag an edge or a corner of the small window that
  plays while you are on another desktop: it grows or shrinks in 16:9, the pointer showing
  where it resizes, and it opens next time where you left it, at that size.
