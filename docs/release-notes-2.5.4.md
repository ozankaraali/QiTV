# QiTV 2.5.4

**Download QiTV from [qitv.app](https://qitv.app), which picks the right build for your
computer, or from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases). Please don't use
others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## New

- **Watch on your phone.** The phone's channel list (the Remote tab's Channels) now says where a
  tap plays: TV or Phone. On Phone, a player sits above the list and the channel you tap plays
  on the phone, with its own full screen and AirPlay; tap TV to hand it back to the TV. More →
  Watch on this phone brings over what the TV is playing. QiTV plays on one screen at a time, so
  your provider counts one stream: the TV lets go when the phone starts, and the phone stops when
  the TV plays again. Away from home, a stream the page can't play inside it opens in the phone's
  own player. Xtream channels go to the phone as HLS, which iPhones play; MKV films and some
  playlist streams may not play on a phone.
- **Find any channel from the list.** "Find a channel" on the phone now finds every channel, the
  open group's own first, so a channel in another group is a search away.

## Fixed

- **No "download" question at home.** When QiTV turned the phone's browser away at its own
  address (for example a phone on a VPN), Safari asked to download a file named after the
  computer's address. The phone now goes straight back to the relay and keeps working there.
