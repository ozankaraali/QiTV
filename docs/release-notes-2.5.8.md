# QiTV 2.5.8

**Download QiTV from [qitv.app](https://qitv.app), which picks the right build for your
computer, or from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases). Please don't use
others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## New

- **Search on the phone without touching the TV.** The phone's Search is now its own: what you
  type is searched on the computer for the phone alone (every provider's channels, films and
  series), and the TV's picture stays as it is. Tap a result to play it, on the TV, or on the
  phone when you watch there. Turn on **Show on TV** under the search box when the room should
  see the search too.
- **QiTV's own finds on the phone.** The phone's "Find a channel" and the Library's find now use
  the computer's search: after what's in the open group or genre, they find channels, genres,
  films and series of every provider the way QiTV does on the computer, whole words first and
  your own provider and recently watched channels before the rest. They read the providers'
  saved lists, so no provider signs in for it.
- **The phone remembers how far you got.** A film or an episode watched on the phone is saved
  like one watched on the TV: tap TV and it goes on from there, and it shows in the Library's
  Continue.

## Fixed

- **TV from the guide.** The phone's TV button now closes the TV's guide or channel list and goes
  back to the picture; before, only Back did.
