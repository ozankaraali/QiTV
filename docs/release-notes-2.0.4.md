# QiTV 2.0.4

Free channels are one click away again, and the mini player and fullscreen buttons show the way
back.

**Please always download QiTV from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases)
instead of using others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## Free channels

- **Get the free channels back in one click.** QiTV starts with iptv-org's
  [publicly available playlist](https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv). If you deleted it, the new
  **Free channels…** button adds it again. It's on the welcome card (shown when you have no
  provider) and in the channel list's sources column, under **＋ Add provider**.
- **All countries or just one.** Choose **All countries**, your own region's list, or
  **Another country…** to pick from about 190 countries (type a few letters to jump in the list).
  The list is added as a provider and loads at once. If you already have it, QiTV switches to it.

## Player controls

- **The way back shows on the button.** In the mini player, its button now shows an arrow out of
  the corner ("Back to the full window", <kbd>Alt+P</kbd>). In fullscreen, the fullscreen button
  shows the corners turned inwards ("Exit fullscreen", <kbd>F</kbd>).
