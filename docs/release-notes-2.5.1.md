# QiTV 2.5.1

**Download QiTV from [qitv.app](https://qitv.app), which picks the right build for your
computer, or from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases). Please don't use
others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## Fixed

- **Closing picture in picture from the phone.** While the TV shows a small picture, the
  phone's More says Close picture in picture, and the button closes it whatever the TV shows.
  Before, it read Picture in picture, and while the channel list was open it opened the list's
  highlighted channel instead. Record and Multiview light up there too while the TV records or
  shows a multiview.
- **The phone's Guide follows the TV.** Change channel on the computer and Watching moves to it
  on the phone. A Guide opened from its tab follows the TV's channel to its row, or to where the
  TV's channel list now is; a group you chose, or a search you typed, stays where it is.
