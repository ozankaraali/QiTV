# QiTV 2.4.2

**Please always download QiTV from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases)
instead of using others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## Fixed

- **The floating picture stays where you grab it (Mac).** A press on the small window that plays
  while you are on another desktop no longer brings QiTV forward and swipes back to the
  fullscreen desktop: drag it, resize it, and double-click it (or swipe back) to watch in
  fullscreen again.
- **It floats sooner.** The small window appears as soon as you leave the fullscreen desktop,
  instead of up to a second later.
