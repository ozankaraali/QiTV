# QiTV 2.4.4

**Please always download QiTV from [Releases](https://github.com/ozankaraali/QiTV/releases)
instead of using others' distributions, which may have malware.**

## New

- **Free channels by country.** A new install starts with the free channels grouped by
  country, so you find yours first. A channel of several countries is listed under each of
  them, and once under All.
- **Free channels from the menu and Settings.** The Providers menu ends with Add provider and
  Free channels (all countries, your region, or another country), and Settings → Providers has
  a Free channels… button beside Add Provider.

## Fixed

- **Channel logos that never showed.** Logos kept on some public image sites were refused, and
  QiTV kept the refusal as the logo for good. They load now, and a logo that cannot be read is
  asked for again later.
