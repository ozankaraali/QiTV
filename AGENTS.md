QiTV — Agent Guide and Working Plan

Overview
- Purpose: Maintain a clear, shared plan and conventions for ongoing refactors and fixes.
- Scope: Applies to the entire repository unless a more specific AGENTS.md is present in a subdirectory.

Principles
- Keep the UI responsive: no blocking network or heavy I/O on the UI thread.
- Fix root causes, not symptoms; keep changes small and focused.
- Prefer composition over monoliths; split large modules by responsibility.
- Use consistent logging over prints and propagate errors meaningfully to the UI when needed.
- Write code that’s testable; isolate logic from PySide UI where possible.

Layout
- The root holds only `main.py` (the entry point; `python main.py` runs the app), the three PyInstaller specs, `pyproject.toml`/`uv.lock`/`requirements.txt`, README/AGENTS/LICENSE/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES and the folders (`licenses/` holds the license texts the packaged apps ship).
- `services/`: everything without widgets (providers, catalog, EPG, config, images, live pipeline, sessions, the update check, download and launch; images are decoded to QImage only). `ui/`: the TV window, its overlays, the Library, the settings dialog (`ui/settings_dialog.py`) and the update dialogs (`ui/update_dialogs.py`). `scripts/`: the smoke test, the demo content and README screenshots, the README's key tables and the Linux installer. `tests/`: unittest suites (`uv run python -m unittest discover -s tests`).

Style & Tooling
- Format: black + isort (configured via pre-commit).
- Lint: flake8 (treats most issues as warnings except syntax/undefined names).
- Types: add gradual type hints; keep mypy green on changed modules.
- Logging: use `logging.getLogger(__name__)` rather than `print`.

- 2.1 stay-live stage 4 (`tv/vlc`, a learned target): Auto latency learns per channel how far behind the live edge it can play without VLC running dry, in place of 2.0.2's 1.5 s per starvation or stall. Part A (`services/live/learned_target.py`, a model of its own): `ArrivalModel` reads the live edge tick by tick as batches (the ticks on which it moved, in a row; a flow no faster than it plays is cut every second) and needs the larger of the gaps between batch starts (95th percentile plus half their interquartile range) and the edge's dips below its own mean (95th percentile of the deepest of each 30 s), plus 1 s for VLC; a gap far beyond the source's rhythm (a stall) is left out, and until it has seen 20 batches (three of them after a still edge, unless the source flows) the need stays at least what the channel taught (or the burst; until round 1 of 4b's review the burst + 1 s). `StarvationPrior` believes how often VLC would run dry with no margin (each starvation weighed by the margin in force, a near-miss a part of one), the margin being 1 s per halving of that rate down to one every two hours; after 20 minutes of quiet playing (a stall VLC got through renews it) the margin halves every 30. The target is max(Normal's 2.5 s, the burst, the need) + the margin, at most max(10 s, max(burst, need) + 2 s) (ruling R37: a segmented source never goes under its burst, as in 2.0.2, and its stall was trouble at once until round 1 of 4b's review, which made every stall a note; `SEGMENT_FLOOR` off is the learned target alone), kept per channel in `live_tuning.json` (`Tuning.learned`: the need, the prior's counts and its hold; a 2.0.2 `extra` reads as a prior that needed that much). Part B (`LiveSession._steer_learned` and `_feed_learned`, by the integration contract of part A's review): each tune under Auto makes one from what the channel taught and feeds it the live edge every tick (its arrivals restart only with another stream from the helper, never on a VLC reopen), a stall once as it begins (with the burst the stall bound used), VLC running dry at the live edge once per dry run, a near-miss before each batch (VLC's headroom less its 0.3 s of caching), a stall VLC got through (its least headroom) and the time played at the edge; trouble counts only while stay-live is LIVE and once it ran out a cushion, judged before the target rises with it. Stay-live steers by it through `SteeredTarget`: a rise in a step of 0.5 s or more once it held 10 s, at once with trouble (and its owed slow-down), a fall at most Auto's quiet pace (0.5 s per 5 minutes; since round 1 of 4b's review only for what trouble raised), never a step down; a learned rise within the band is drift and builds nothing. The steering target reads the source's burst as the lag filter has it, not the helper's one-off backlog "burst" (since round 1 of 4b's review the timeline's, Go live's, the start's and the cushion's add the helper's burst at once). A move of 0.5 s or more is said at INFO ("arrivals", "trouble", "quiet", since round 1 also "the source's burst"; no names or URLs) and saved, as are trouble and the tune's end (without a 2.0.2 `extra`, leaving the rate lock and direct play as the store has them). "· adjusted" shows while the prior's margin is above zero (trouble holds the target up), not for what the arrivals alone need. QITV_SEGMENT_FLOOR=0 in the environment turns the burst floor off for a run (read once at start; for the A/B, not a setting). Conventions for New Code
- Keep UI and data/services separate. Long-running network calls must run in QThread.
- Avoid coupling VLC/player code to UI state more than necessary; use signals.
- Prefer dataclasses or typed dicts for structured data passed between layers.
 - Never create Qt GUI objects (QPixmap/QIcon) or start timers from worker threads; emit plain data and build UI in the main thread.
